Win rates are all-league Ranked data from Brawl Planet, — ranked maps loaded, newest match in the sample —. Retired maps with no ranked data are left out. The Maps tab ranks your brawlers on any one map and lists who counters each of them. Two axes, never blended: Map is the brawler's win rate on this map. Matchup is the average of its win rate against each enemy brawler already on the board, using only pairings Brawl Planet publishes; with no published pairing it shows "no data" rather than a substitute number. Each pairing is taken from the most specific source available: this mode on the brawler's own page, this mode on the enemy's page (flipped, since one side's win is the other's loss), then the all-modes numbers the same way. Per-mode lists exist only where a brawler has enough matches in that mode, so some pairings fall back to all-modes numbers. Pairing data is Diamond 1 and above, pooled across leagues, refreshed hourly by the site. Status compares your pick only against brawlers you could still have taken. "Better pick" names one that was at least as good on both axes and better on one. A good pick is one nothing beat that way; there can be more than one, and choosing between them is a judgement about how much the matchup matters. Where either brawler has no matchup number, the comparison is on map alone. At pick 1 the board is empty, so it is map only. Grading only considers brawlers you own at Level 11 plus this season's free four — it answers "was this the best pick available to you", not "the best pick in the game".
